# Consent form: Unhurried benchmark recordings

Thank you for helping. Please read this before we record.

## What is this for?

Unhurried is a tool that helps voice assistants wait for people who stammer,
instead of cutting them off. We test it with short recordings of people
reading booking requests, like "Book a table for two at seven on Friday".

The test measures **the computer, not you**: how often the assistant cuts a
person off, and how often it understands the request. We never score or rate
your speech.

## What we record

- About 5 to 10 short clips of your voice (each under 20 seconds).
- Only your voice. No video. No name is stored with the audio.

## Who hears the audio?

- **Your audio stays private.** It is kept only on the project owner's
  computer. It is **not** put in the public code repository, the demo video,
  or the hackathon submission.
- To run the test, the audio is sent to Meta's speech service (Muse Voice
  Transcribe) to turn it into text.
- **Only the results are published**, as counts, for example "the assistant
  cut off 3 of 30 clips". Your results cannot be linked to you.

Why: the hackathon rules give the organizers broad rights to use everything
in the submission, including voices. We do not want your voice to be part of
that.

## How to ask for removal

Send a message to the project owner at any time, before or after the
hackathon. We will delete your recordings and remove your results from any
future version of the results. You do not need to give a reason.

## Your choice

You can stop at any time, skip any line, or ask to delete a clip right after
recording it.
