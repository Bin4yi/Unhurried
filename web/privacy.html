Privacy

What Unhurried keeps, and what it does not.

Your voice

Live audio is not stored. It is streamed to Meta's speech service (Muse Voice Transcribe) to turn it into text, and then it is gone. Only the text of the conversation is kept in the session log on the server, and you can delete it.

Voice Passport

Your personal profile is encrypted in your browser before it is saved (AES-256-GCM, with a key made from your passphrase using PBKDF2, 600,000 rounds). The server stores only the locked file. It does not have your passphrase and cannot read it.

The profile holds: how long your pauses inside a sentence usually are, words you want Unhurried to listen for, word switches you approved, and your settings. It never holds audio, and it never holds a score or rating of your speech.

You can export it as a file, import it on another device, or delete it with one button.

What leaves your browser during a session

When a session starts, your browser unlocks the profile and sends only what this session needs to the Unhurried server: your maximum wait time, your word list, approved word switches and settings. The server keeps them in memory and forgets them at the end.

Honest limit: your word list is sent to Meta's speech service during the session, so it can listen for those words (keyword biasing). The text of what you say is sent to Meta's language model (Muse Spark) to check if a sentence is finished, to clean up repeated sounds and corrections, and to run the demo booking assistant.

Camera

Unhurried does not use your camera.
